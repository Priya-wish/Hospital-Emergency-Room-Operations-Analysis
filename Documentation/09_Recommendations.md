# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are proposed:

## Improve Emergency Response Time

Increase staffing and optimize triage during busy periods to improve the percentage of patients seen within 30 minutes.

---

## Optimize Resource Allocation

Allocate additional resources to General Practice and Orthopedics, as these departments receive the highest number of referrals.

---

## Monitor Patient Flow

Regularly monitor admission rates and patient arrival trends to improve bed management and operational efficiency.

---

## Enhance Patient Experience

Reduce waiting times and continue tracking satisfaction scores to improve overall patient experience.

---

## Continue Performance Monitoring

Use the dashboard regularly to monitor key operational metrics and support data-driven decision-making.
