# Business Insights

## Insight 1

A total of approximately 9,000 patients visited the Emergency Room during the analysis period.

---

## Insight 2

Around 50% of patients required hospital admission, while the remaining patients were treated and discharged.

---

## Insight 3

The average patient waiting time was approximately 35 minutes, with only about 40% of patients being seen within the target time of 30 minutes.

---

## Insight 4

Most patients did not require specialist referrals. Among referrals, General Practice and Orthopedics handled the highest patient volume.

---

## Insight 5

Patient visits remained relatively stable over time without significant seasonal fluctuations.

---

## Insight 6

The gender distribution was nearly balanced, and younger age groups accounted for the highest number of Emergency Room visits.
